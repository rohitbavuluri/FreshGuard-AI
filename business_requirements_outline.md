# Business Requirements — Draft Outline

## Business problem
Grocers may lose money when perishable stock expires before sale.

## Target users
Store managers, inventory clerks, cashiers.

## User stories
- As a manager, I want expiry alerts so that I can act before stock spoils.
- As a cashier, I want to record sales so that stock counts stay accurate.
- As a manager, I want waste estimates so that I can decide on discounts.

## KPIs
Estimated at-risk inventory cost, expired units, stock accuracy, percentage of items discounted before expiry.

## Constraints
Sample data only; no real POS integration; forecasts are illustrative, not validated ML predictions.

## Privacy and security
No customer personal information; prototype has no authentication and is not production-ready.
